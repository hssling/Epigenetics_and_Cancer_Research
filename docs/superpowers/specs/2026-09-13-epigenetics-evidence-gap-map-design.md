## 1. Background and rationale

### 1.1 Why this project exists

Epigenetic mechanisms sit at the interface between modifiable exposure and health outcome. They are, in principle, the molecular substrate through which nutrition, physical activity, tobacco, pollution, and psychosocial stress translate into disease risk — and, being reversible, they are in principle modifiable. A parallel literature uses epigenetic state as a *measurement*: methylation biomarkers for cancer screening, epigenetic clocks for risk stratification, cfDNA methylation for prognosis.

Despite two decades of work, there is no map of which of these approaches are actually ready for health or public health practice, and where in the care pathway they apply. Practitioners cannot tell dense evidence from thin, or mechanistic plausibility from demonstrated benefit.

### 1.2 Relationship to prior work in this repository

This project supersedes two earlier attempts in this repository. Both are quarantined, not extended.

The root pipeline (`scripts/`, `output/Epigenetics_PublicHealth_Manuscript.md`) produced a manuscript whose reported findings are not derived from data:

- `proportion_positive`, `sensitivity`, and `specificity` were generated by `random.random()` and `random.uniform()` (`scripts/fetch_pubmed_data.py:293-296`). The manuscript's reported "mean positive detection 48.9%" is the mean of a uniform random number generator; the measured value across 616 rows is 0.489.
- 424 of 616 effect sizes (69%) are the hardcoded constant `0.3`; 468 of 616 sample sizes (76%) are the constant `200` (`scripts/prepare_master_dataset.py:88,92`).
- Confidence intervals were computed as `effect x 0.8` to `effect x 1.2` — arithmetic, with no variance input.
- `data/prisma_counts.csv` records 616 identified, 0 excluded, 616 included. No screening occurred.

The `re_research_2025/` module uses real, verifiable data (29 records, all reconciling arithmetically), but is described as a systematic review when it is an unscreened bibliometric snapshot: the PubMed query returned exactly 29 records against a `retmax` of 100, and all 29 were analysed. Its keyword classifier is first-match and misclassifies — an exposome/environmental paper and a prostate gene-expression paper are both coded "Nutritional" because the nutrition keyword block is tested first.

These outputs were never submitted, shared, or cited. Remediation is therefore housekeeping (Section 9.4), not formal correction.

The failure mode is recorded here because the design below is shaped around preventing its recurrence. The root cause was not carelessness but an inverted dependency: the pipeline committed to producing a "network meta-analysis" as an output format, then substituted placeholder values whenever the source data could not fill that format. Section 8 encodes the structural fix.

## 2. Objectives

**Primary.** To map the available human evidence on epigenetic approaches — both interventions acting through the epigenome and epigenetic measurements guiding care — across the full continuum of health and public health practice, identifying where evidence is dense, where it is sparse, and where it is methodologically trustworthy.

**Secondary.**

1. To quantify the completeness of the causal chain in the existing literature (Section 4.2), establishing how much evidence connects intervention to epigenetic change *and* to health outcome.
2. To pool effect estimates in any cell of the map meeting pre-specified poolability criteria (Section 7.3).
3. To produce a research-priority statement derived from mapped absence, not from narrative assertion.

## 3. Deliverables

Three sequential outputs, each independently publishable. Each is gated on its predecessor.

| # | Deliverable | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Registered protocol (PROSPERO, or OSF Registries if PROSPERO declines an EGM) | Must be registered and timestamped **before** any screening begins |
| 2 | Evidence gap map | Primary paper |
| 3 | Nested meta-analysis | Scope determined **after** coding, by the rule in Section 7.3. May legitimately yield nothing |

Deliverable 3 is not promised in advance. The protocol pre-specifies the *rule* for identifying a poolable cell, not the cell itself. If no cell qualifies, that is reported as a pre-registered null finding.

## 4. Scope and eligibility

### 4.1 Inclusion criteria

| Dimension | Criterion |
|---|---|
| Population | Humans, any age, any condition, any country |
| Conditions | All health conditions (disease-agnostic; organised by approach, not by disease) |
| Epigenetic marker | **Must be directly measured.** DNA methylation (global, candidate-gene, or epigenome-wide), histone modification, chromatin state, or epigenetic clock / DNAm age |
| Approach | A modifiable exposure or intervention, **or** an epigenetic measurement used to inform a clinical or public health decision |
| Design | Primary studies: RCT, non-randomised trial, cohort, case-control, cross-sectional |
| Date | 2015-01-01 to 2026-12-31 |
| Language | English |

### 4.2 The chain-completeness requirement

Every claim in this field is a three-link chain:

```
Exposure / Intervention  -->  Epigenetic change  -->  Health outcome
         (A)                        (B)                     (C)
```

Studies rarely measure all three. Eligibility requires a measured epigenetic marker (link B), and **chain completeness is coded as a mapped variable**, not used as an exclusion:

- **A->B** — intervention or exposure shown to alter an epigenetic marker. Mechanistic. Abundant.
- **B->C** — epigenetic marker shown to predict or associate with a health outcome. Prognostic. Abundant.
- **A->B->C** — intervention alters the marker *and* that change relates to a health outcome. The only design supporting a claim that an epigenetic approach works. Expected to be rare.

Making this a mapped variable rather than a filter is a deliberate design decision. It preserves the mechanistic and prognostic literature as mapped context while making the translational gap the map's central, quantified finding. Gap maps exist to report absence as a result; this is the mechanism by which this map does so.

### 4.3 Exclusion criteria

- Animal, in vitro, or cell-line only studies
- Reviews, systematic reviews, meta-analyses, editorials, comments, letters, conference abstracts without data
- No directly measured epigenetic marker (this excludes intervention trials assumed to act epigenetically without measurement)
- Non-coding RNA as the sole epigenetic marker (see 4.4)

### 4.4 Non-coding RNA exclusion

miRNA and lncRNA are excluded as sole eligible markers. Rationale:

1. **Definitional.** ncRNA regulation is post-transcriptional and not a heritable chromatin state; strict definitions of epigenetics exclude it.
2. **Empirical.** Including ncRNA raises the PubMed corpus from 11,965 to 21,528 records (Section 5.2) — a near-doubling of screening burden.
3. **Yield.** The majority of additional records are mechanistic cancer-biology studies expected to fail the A->B / B->C eligibility test.

This boundary is stated explicitly in the protocol and the manuscript's limitations as a scoping decision, not an oversight. Studies measuring ncRNA *alongside* an eligible marker remain eligible on the strength of that eligible marker.

### 4.5 Date window rationale

2015-2026 brackets the era of mature array-based EWAS (Illumina 450K/EPIC) and validated epigenetic clocks. Earlier work is dominated by candidate-gene studies on obsolete platforms whose findings are largely non-replicable and non-comparable. The window reduces the PubMed corpus from 11,965 to 9,089.

## 5. Search strategy

### 5.1 Sources

**Bibliographic databases:** PubMed/MEDLINE (primary, via NCBI E-utilities), Embase, Web of Science Core Collection, Cochrane CENTRAL.

**Trial registries:** ClinicalTrials.gov, WHO ICTRP. These serve a dual purpose — identifying unpublished and ongoing studies, and populating a "registered but unreported" overlay on the map that indicates where evidence is in flight.

**Supplementary:** forward and backward citation chasing on all included studies; hand-search of the three highest-yield journals identified after screening.

### 5.2 Concept blocks and validated record counts

Counts below were obtained against live PubMed on 2026-09-13 and are recorded so the design is sized against measured, not estimated, burden.

### 5.3 Strategy quality assurance

- Search strategy peer-reviewed against the **PRESS 2015** checklist, by an information specialist or librarian where available.
- Every query string, database, execution date, and raw count committed to the repository as a dated artefact.
- Deduplication by DOI, then PMID, then fuzzy match on title + year + first author, with counts recorded at each step to populate the PRISMA diagram.

## 6. The map

### 6.1 Primary view: approach x care stage

**Rows (10 approaches).** Interventions: nutrition and dietary patterns; physical activity and sedentary behaviour; tobacco and alcohol; environmental exposure reduction; psychosocial, stress and sleep; perinatal and early-life (DOHaD); pharmacological epigenetic agents. Tools: methylation screening and early-detection tests; epigenetic clocks and ageing biomarkers; molecular classifiers for treatment selection and monitoring.

**Columns (7 care stages).** Health promotion; primary prevention; screening and early detection; risk stratification; treatment and disease modification; prognosis and monitoring; recovery, survivorship and rehabilitation.

**Visual encoding.** Bubble size = study count. Cell colour = composite epigenetic-quality tier (Section 7.2). Chain-completeness shown as an overlay. Registry-only studies shown as a distinct marker.

The row set is provisional. Pilot screening (Section 7.1, Stage 0) will confirm whether any row is empty or requires splitting; changes are recorded in the protocol amendments log.

### 6.2 Secondary view: approach x condition domain

The conventional intervention x outcome layout used by 3ie and Campbell EGMs. Columns: cardiometabolic; oncological; neurological and mental health; respiratory; perinatal; immune and infectious; ageing and mortality; other.

This is generated as a pivot of the same coded dataset at zero additional coding cost, and is included because it is the layout reviewers recognise.

### 6.3 Design principle: dimensions, not displays

The coded dataset stores each dimension as an independent field. No display format is baked into the data. Every map variant — including the chain-completeness cross-tabulation — is a grouping operation over the same table.

This directly addresses the prior pipeline's failure, where the output shape was fixed in advance and data was bent to fit it.

## 7. Methods

### 7.1 Screening

The investigator is the sole human reviewer, working with AI assistance. The design allocates human attention to errors that are unrecoverable, and relies on workflow structure to catch errors that are.

**Stage 0 — Calibration.** 300 randomly selected records. The investigator screens all 300 blind; the AI screens the same 300 independently. Cohen's kappa computed, all disagreements inspected, eligibility criteria and AI prompt refined. Repeated until kappa >= 0.75. The final kappa is reported in the manuscript.

**Stage 1 — AI title/abstract screening** of the full corpus. Structured output per record: decision (`include` / `exclude` / `unclear`), reason, and confidence score. All `unclear` records route to the investigator.

**Stage 2 — Validation, weighted to false negatives.**

False positives are self-correcting: every AI-include proceeds to full text, where the investigator assesses it directly. The unrecoverable error is a false negative — an eligible study excluded at title/abstract and never seen again.

Validation therefore targets AI-**excludes**: a 1,000-record sample, stratified by AI confidence to oversample low-confidence exclusions, hand-screened blind by the investigator.

This yields a quantified recall estimate with a confidence interval. If zero false negatives are found in 1,000, the exact (Clopper-Pearson) two-sided 95% upper bound on the false-negative rate is 0.368% — equivalently, a one-sided 95% upper bound of 0.299%. Against an expected 10,500-14,500 exclusions this supports a published statement that at most approximately 39-53 eligible studies were plausibly missed (two-sided bound; 31-43 one-sided). The manuscript reports the two-sided bound as the conservative figure.

If false negatives *are* found, the observed rate and its exact CI are reported as-is, and the sample is extended to tighten the estimate. A recall estimate that comes out poor is reported, not re-run until favourable.

This is a stronger and more auditable transparency claim than conventional dual-human review, which typically does not quantify its own recall at all.

Reported screening metrics: Cohen's kappa (calibration set), sensitivity/recall with 95% CI, specificity, and the full confusion matrix.

**Stage 3 — Full text.** Burden is split by deliverable:

- *Gap-map coding:* AI extracts the coded fields from full text. The investigator verifies a stratified sample plus every study in any cell driving a headline claim. This is accepted EGM practice — the task is assigning seven categorical fields, not extracting outcome data.
- *Meta-analysis extraction:* full manual extraction by the investigator, without exception. This set is expected to be 30-60 studies. Pooled estimates are where extraction error causes real harm.

**Reproducibility.** All AI prompts, model identifiers, versions, and run timestamps are committed as artefacts.

### 7.2 Risk of bias and study quality

Tool selected by coded `modality`:

| Modality | Tool |
|---|---|
| Randomised interventions | Cochrane RoB 2 |
| Non-randomised interventions | ROBINS-I |
| Diagnostic accuracy | QUADAS-2 |
| Prognostic models | PROBAST |
| Certainty of pooled findings | GRADE (meta-analysis only) |

**Epigenetic methods quality checklist**, applied to every included study. Standard risk-of-bias tools do not capture epigenetics-specific validity threats:

1. Cell-type composition adjusted for, or purified cell populations used?
2. Batch effects corrected, and the correction stated?
3. Multiple-testing correction appropriate to the platform?
4. Independent replication cohort?
5. Tissue relevance justified (is the sampled tissue a defensible proxy for the target tissue)?
6. Platform and normalisation pipeline reported?

Unadjusted cell composition is the field's classic confounder: an apparent methylation difference in blood that is in fact a shift in leukocyte proportions. Mapping this lets the map distinguish where evidence exists from where it is trustworthy.

All six items are stored as **separate flags**, and a **composite tier** is derived from them. Flags support transparency and reader re-weighting; the composite drives cell colour in the map. Both are retained.

### 7.3 Meta-analysis feasibility gate

Pre-specified in the protocol; applied only after coding is complete.

> A cell is poolable if it contains **>= 10 studies** that share a **modality**, have a **comparable comparator**, report an **extractable common effect metric with variance**, and are **not uniformly at high risk of bias**.

Cells passing the gate are pooled as follows:

- **Intervention effects:** random-effects meta-analysis (REML) with Hartung-Knapp adjustment. Report I-squared and **prediction intervals** alongside confidence intervals. Egger's test for small-study effects where >= 10 studies. Leave-one-out sensitivity analysis.
- **Diagnostic accuracy:** bivariate random-effects or HSROC models. Naive independent pooling of sensitivity and specificity is not used.

**Network meta-analysis is not planned.** It will be undertaken only if a genuinely connected network with shared comparators emerges from the coded data, and only with formal transitivity assessment. Pre-committing against NMA is deliberate: the prior pipeline named the method before seeing the data and then manufactured data to suit it.

If no cell passes the gate, this is reported as a pre-registered null finding. That is a legitimate result.

## 8. Technical architecture

### 8.1 Repository structure

New tree. The existing `scripts/`, `output/`, and `re_research_2025/` directories are quarantined (Section 9.4), not extended.

```
egm/
|-- protocol/      registered protocol, amendments log
|-- search/        query strings, raw exports, dedup log, PRISMA counts
|-- screening/     AI prompts + versions, decisions, validation sample, kappa
|-- coding/        coded dataset (one row per study), codebook, verification log
|-- analysis/      map generation, feasibility gate, pooling
|-- outputs/       figures, tables, manuscript
+-- tests/         data-integrity suite
```

Data layers are separated and raw exports are append-only: raw -> deduplicated -> screened -> coded -> analysed.

### 8.2 Coding schema

One row per study. Dimensions stored independently of any display format.

| Field | Values / type |
|---|---|
| `study_id`, `pmid`, `doi` | identifiers |
| `approach` | one of the 10 row categories |
| `care_stage` | one of the 7 care stages |
| `condition_domain` | one of the 8 condition domains |
| `modality` | intervention-effect / diagnostic-accuracy / prognostic |
| `chain_link` | A->B / B->C / A->B->C |
| `marker_class` | methylation (global / candidate / EWAS) / histone / chromatin / clock |
| `design` | RCT / non-randomised trial / cohort / case-control / cross-sectional |
| `n`, `population`, `country`, `follow_up` | nullable |
| `effect_metric`, `effect_value`, `effect_variance` | **nullable — no defaults** |
| `rob_tool`, `rob_domain_*`, `rob_overall` | per modality |
| `epi_quality_1..6`, `epi_quality_tier` | six flags plus derived composite |
| `source_location` | page / table / figure for every quantitative value |

### 8.3 Data integrity rules

Four rules, enforced in code, each corresponding to a specific prior failure:

1. **No default values.** Missing data stays `NULL` and propagates as `NULL`. Any function substituting a constant for absent data is a defect. *(Prior failure: `effect = 0.3`, `population = 200`.)*
2. **Fail loud.** A missing required field raises an exception; it is never silently filled. *(Prior failure: placeholders introduced explicitly so downstream R scripts would not fail on missing data.)*
3. **No RNG in the data path.** *(Prior failure: `random.random()` supplying reported outcome values.)*
4. **Single source of truth.** Every number appearing in the manuscript is injected from the coded dataset at build time. No hand-typed statistics. *(Prevents figure/caption/text drift.)*

### 8.4 Test suite

Tests are written before the pipeline components they guard (TDD).

- **Placeholder detection:** assert no numeric field has a modal value exceeding 20% frequency — the exact signature of the prior dataset, where `0.3` occupied 69% of one column and `200` occupied 76% of another.
- **RNG absence:** assert `random` / `numpy.random` is not imported anywhere in the data path outside the validation-sampling module.
- **Provenance:** assert every non-null quantitative value carries a `source_location`.
- **PRISMA reconciliation:** assert identified − excluded = included at every stage, and that total exclusions are non-zero.
- **Manuscript consistency:** assert every percentage and count in the manuscript recomputes from the coded dataset within rounding tolerance.
- **Reference integrity:** assert every cited PMID resolves and appears in the coded dataset.

## 9. Governance

### 9.1 Registration

The protocol is registered and timestamped before any screening begins. PROSPERO for the review component; OSF Registries as fallback for the map component, as PROSPERO does not consistently accept evidence gap maps. Registration ID recorded in the manuscript.

### 9.2 Reporting standards

PRISMA 2020 (flow and checklist), PRISMA-S (search reporting), and Campbell Collaboration EGM guidance for map construction and presentation.

### 9.3 AI use disclosure and authorship

An ICMJE-compliant statement in the Methods specifies precisely which tasks were AI-assisted (title/abstract screening, full-text field extraction), which model and version, and the measured validation metrics from Section 7.1.

AI systems are not authors. The existing `re_research_2025/AUTHORS` file lists "Epigenetics Research Automation Agent (Google DeepMind)" under Research Team; this entry is removed (it also names the wrong vendor — the tooling is Anthropic-based).

### 9.4 Remediation of prior outputs

The prior outputs were never submitted, shared, or cited, so no formal correction is required. Required housekeeping before new work is published from this repository:

1. A prominent `DEPRECATED.md` notice on the root pipeline and its outputs, stating that reported values were pipeline artefacts — including placeholder constants and random number generation — and are not research findings.
2. Removal or disabling of the "Living Review Pipeline" badge and the scheduled GitHub Action, which currently regenerates and commits fabricated outputs weekly.
3. Retention of the code and data for transparency, clearly labelled, rather than deletion.

Reviewers of the new work may find the old repository; it should be unambiguous on inspection that the prior artefacts are superseded and were never presented as findings.

## 10. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Corpus larger than estimated after multi-database dedup | Counts in 5.2 are measured, not estimated. If dedup yields >20,000, tighten the date window to 2018-2026 as a pre-specified contingency recorded in the protocol |
| AI screening recall below acceptable threshold | Stage 0 gate (kappa >= 0.75) must pass before Stage 1 proceeds. If it cannot be reached after prompt refinement, scope narrows to the exposure arm only (4,029 records), which is single-reviewer feasible |
| No cell passes the meta-analysis feasibility gate | Pre-registered as a legitimate null. The gap map stands alone as deliverable 2 |
| Full-text screening burden exceeds solo capacity | Gap-map coding is AI-assisted with sampled verification (7.1 Stage 3). Only the much smaller meta-analysis set requires full manual extraction |
| Provisional approach rows prove empty or over-broad | Confirmed during Stage 0 pilot; changes recorded in the protocol amendments log rather than made silently |
| PROSPERO declines the EGM registration | OSF Registries fallback, specified in advance |

## 12. Decision log

Decisions taken during design, with the alternatives rejected.

| Decision | Rejected alternative | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| All conditions, approach-led organisation | Cancer-only; NCDs-only | Matches the stated practice-facing question; disease becomes a coded filter rather than a scope boundary |
| Interventions and biomarkers in one map with a `modality` axis | Two separate maps; either alone | Answers the full care-continuum question in one coherent paper while keeping incompatible evidence types interpretable via per-modality RoB tools |
| Measured marker required; chain completeness mapped | Marker + outcome both required; mechanism-plausible with marker optional | Keeps the corpus defensibly epigenetic while making the translational gap the headline finding rather than an exclusion |
| ncRNA excluded | Included | Definitional, plus a measured near-doubling of corpus for low expected yield |
| 2015-2026 window | Open-ended; 2019-2025 | Brackets mature EWAS platforms and validated clocks; excludes non-replicable candidate-gene era |
| Solo + AI screening, validation weighted to false negatives | Dual human; dual AI; narrowed scope | Matches available resource; false-positive errors self-correct at full text, so human attention goes where errors are unrecoverable |
| Approach x care stage primary, approach x condition secondary | Either alone; care stage x chain completeness | Practice-facing headline plus conventional layout, from one coded dataset at no extra cost |
| Epigenetic quality as six flags plus derived composite | Composite only; flags only | Flags give transparency and re-weighting; composite is needed for cell colour encoding |
| Meta-analysis scope decided post-coding by pre-specified rule | Scope named in the protocol | Feasibility cannot be known before the map exists; naming the method first is the specific error that produced the prior fabricated output |
